# Weekly Exec Digest

**Every department's week, in numbers and one paragraph each.**

The digest counts what happened in each department - workflow runs and how
they ended, agent conversations, reviews waiting on people, model calls and
their cost - and writes a short summary per department plus a company view.
It runs every Friday afternoon on its own, or whenever you ask.

## How it works

1. **Count.** For each department, the week's activity is read from the
   platform's own records. The numbers are counted in code, not estimated.
2. **Summarize.** One writer per department turns its numbers into a
   headline, two to four sentences, and up to three things to follow up.
3. **Compose.** The company summary leads with what the executive team
   should know and the follow-ups that matter most.

## What you get

- A table of every department's week and the company totals.
- A paragraph and a watch list per department.
- A PDF, Word or Markdown copy to send on.

Only counts cross departments. No conversation or document content leaves
the department it belongs to.
