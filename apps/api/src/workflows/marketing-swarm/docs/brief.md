# Marketing Swarm

**Several writers, one brief, scored the same way, and you pick the winner.**

Give the swarm a brief. Each writer you choose drafts it in its own voice and
on its own model. Every draft is scored on the same facets (hook, clarity,
call to action, claims backed by evidence, and so on). Editors send drafts
back with coaching until they pass. Evaluators rank the finalists, and you
pick the one to use.

## How it works

1. **Write.** Each writer drafts the brief in its persona, on its model.
2. **Score.** Jev answers each facet's question for each draft. Length is
   measured against the content type.
3. **Edit.** Each editor weights the facets it cares about. A draft that
   reaches every editor's bar is approved. One that falls short goes back:
   a coach turns its weakest facets into concrete feedback, and the writer
   revises. This repeats up to the number of rewrites you allow.
4. **Rank.** Each evaluator ranks the final drafts by its own weighting. The
   standings are the mean place across evaluators.
5. **Pick.** You approve the leader, skip or polish finalists, or decline
   them all.

## Why it helps

Models write; they do not grade themselves. Every score is a typed answer to
a narrow question, and every verdict is arithmetic you can read, so "why did
this one win?" has an answer. Marketing tunes the editors, evaluators and
writers in the workflow's admin, without a deploy.
