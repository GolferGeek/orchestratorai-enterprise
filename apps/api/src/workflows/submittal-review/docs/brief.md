# Submittal Review

**A contractor's submittal, checked against the specification, line by line.**

Pick the specification section and upload (or paste) the contractor's
product data. The review lists every requirement in the section that a
submittal can show - materials, ratings, values, standards, warranties -
and checks the submittal against each, quoting its evidence. Jev then
confirms each quote really is in the submittal, so a finding never rests on
words the submittal does not contain. You confirm or correct the findings,
and the response letter is drafted.

## How it works

1. **Requirements.** An agent lists the section's checkable requirements,
   keeping every value and standard exactly as written.
2. **Evaluate.** One evaluator per requirement: compliant, deviation or
   missing, with the submittal's own words as evidence.
3. **Verify.** Jev's citation-in-record rubric checks every quoted piece of
   evidence against the submittal text.
4. **Review.** You confirm the findings or correct any of them.
5. **Respond.** The action follows a rule - anything open means revise and
   resubmit; deviations you accept mean approved as noted - and a writer
   drafts the letter.

## What you get

- A findings table with evidence and verification.
- The action and the response letter, ready to send.
- Deviations and missing items on the Issues tab.
