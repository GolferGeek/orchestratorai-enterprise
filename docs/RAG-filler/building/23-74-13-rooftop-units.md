# Section 23 74 13 - Packaged Rooftop Air-Conditioning Units

## 1.3 Submittals
A. Product data with performance at design conditions, electrical data, weights and curb details.
B. Sound data. C. Factory start-up and warranty information.

## 2.1 Units
A. Factory-assembled units, **nominal 15-ton cooling capacity**, with gas heat.
B. Efficiency: **IEER of 14.0 or higher** (ASHRAE 90.1-2019 minimum for this size is lower; this project exceeds it).
C. Refrigerant: **R-454B** or other refrigerant with GWP under 700. R-410A is **not acceptable**.
D. Economizer with fault detection and diagnostics (FDD) per California Title 24 style requirements.
E. Supply fan: direct-drive EC motor with variable speed.

## 2.3 Electrical
A. 460V / 3-phase / 60 Hz, single-point power connection with factory disconnect.

## 1.6 Warranty
A. Five years on compressors; one year on parts.
