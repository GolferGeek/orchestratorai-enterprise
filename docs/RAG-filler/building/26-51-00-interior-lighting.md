# Section 26 51 00 - Interior Lighting

## 1.3 Submittals
A. Product data for each luminaire type in the fixture schedule. B. Photometric data. C. Driver and control compatibility.

## 2.1 Luminaires
A. LED luminaires with **color temperature 3500K** and **CRI of 90 or higher**.
B. Efficacy of at least 110 lumens per watt for linear fixtures.
C. Rated life L70 of **60,000 hours** minimum.
D. 0-10V dimming to 1%.

## 2.3 Controls
A. Occupancy sensors in private offices and restrooms; daylight sensors in perimeter zones within 15 feet of windows.
