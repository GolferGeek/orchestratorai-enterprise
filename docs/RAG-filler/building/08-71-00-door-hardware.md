# Section 08 71 00 - Door Hardware

## 1.3 Submittals
A. Hardware schedule by opening, keyed to the door schedule.
B. Product data for each item. C. Keying schedule approved by the Owner.

## 2.2 Hinges
A. Full-mortise butt hinges, ANSI/BHMA A156.1 Grade 1, **4-1/2 x 4-1/2 inch**, stainless steel at exterior doors.

## 2.3 Locksets
A. Mortise locksets, **ANSI/BHMA A156.13 Grade 1**, lever trim meeting ADA (operable without tight grasping or twisting).
B. Cylindrical locksets are **not acceptable**.

## 2.5 Closers
A. Surface-mounted, ANSI/BHMA A156.4 Grade 1, adjustable so that interior doors open with **no more than 5 lbf** of force.

## 2.8 Finishes
A. Satin stainless steel (BHMA 630) throughout unless noted.
