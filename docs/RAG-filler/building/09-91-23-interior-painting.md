# Section 09 91 23 - Interior Painting

## 1.3 Submittals
A. Product data sheet for each coating, showing VOC content.
B. Color samples for Architect's selection.

## 2.1 Materials
A. Coatings shall be from a single manufacturer for each system.
B. Volatile organic compounds: **50 g/L maximum** for flat and non-flat interior paints (Green Seal GS-11).
C. Interior walls, gypsum board: one coat primer and **two finish coats** of acrylic latex, **eggshell** sheen in offices and corridors, **semi-gloss** in restrooms and break rooms.

## 3.2 Application
A. Apply at the manufacturer's recommended spreading rate; minimum dry film thickness per coat 1.2 mils.
B. Do not paint when surface temperature is below 50 F.
