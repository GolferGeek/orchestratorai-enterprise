# Section 07 54 23 - Thermoplastic Polyolefin (TPO) Roofing
Project: Northwind Denver Office Fit-Out and Roof Replacement. Project Manual, Division 07.

## 1.3 Submittals
A. Product data for membrane, insulation, cover board and adhesives.
B. Manufacturer's warranty sample.
C. Installer certification letter from the membrane manufacturer.

## 2.1 Membrane
A. TPO sheet complying with ASTM D6878, **minimum 60 mil (1.5 mm)** thickness, reinforced, white.
B. Solar reflectance index (SRI) of **at least 78** (initial), per ASTM E1980.
C. Fire classification: UL 790 Class A.

## 2.2 Insulation
A. Polyisocyanurate board, ASTM C1289 Type II Class 1, total thermal resistance of **R-30 minimum**, installed in two layers with staggered joints.

## 1.6 Warranty
A. Manufacturer's **20-year no-dollar-limit (NDL)** system warranty covering membrane, insulation and accessories.
B. Installer's 2-year workmanship warranty.

## 3.4 Installation
A. Fully adhered system; mechanically fastened systems are not acceptable.
B. Wind uplift rating FM 1-90 minimum.
