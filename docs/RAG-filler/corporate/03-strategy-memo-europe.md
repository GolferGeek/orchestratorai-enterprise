# Strategy Memo: Expanding in Europe (CEO to the board, September 2026)

## Why now
Thirty-one percent of pipeline is now European, up from 18% a year ago. EU customers increasingly require data residency and local contracting entities; the EU region solves residency, but contracting through the US entity loses deals in Germany and France.

## Proposal
Open a Berlin office in Q1 FY27 with a German GmbH as the contracting entity. Initial team: sales (8), customer success (6), solutions engineering (4), support (5), and a country manager plus an office/people lead.

## Cost
USD 6.8M in FY27 (people USD 5.1M, office USD 0.9M, legal and entity set-up USD 0.4M, travel and events USD 0.4M).

## Risks
- Hiring pace in Berlin for senior sales roles.
- Works-council obligations once headcount passes the threshold.
- Currency exposure on EUR-denominated contracts; Treasury proposes a rolling hedge for 50% of forecast EUR receipts.

## Alternatives considered
An employer-of-record model keeps costs lower in year one but does not give customers a German contracting entity, which is the main reason deals are lost.
