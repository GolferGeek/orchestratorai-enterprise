# Board Meeting - Q3 2026 Summary (Northwind Services, Inc.)

## Headline numbers (quarter ended 2026-09-30)
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR): USD 48.2M, up 31% year over year and 7% quarter over quarter.
- Net revenue retention: 118%. Gross revenue retention: 92%.
- Gross margin: 74% (target 75%). Hosting costs rose after the EU region launch.
- Operating cash burn: USD 2.1M in the quarter; cash on hand USD 61M, runway over 36 months at the current burn.
- Headcount: 312 (plan: 320). Engineering 141, go-to-market 104, G&A 67.

## What went well
- EU region launched on schedule; 22 EU customers migrated, including two of the top ten accounts.
- Enterprise tier closed 9 new logos over USD 250K ARR, against a plan of 6.

## What did not
- Mid-market churn rose to 3.1% monthly in August after the pricing change; win-back offers cut it to 2.4% in September.
- The analytics add-on missed its adoption goal (18% of accounts against 30%).

## Decisions requested of the board
1. Approve the FY27 operating plan draft at USD 64M ending ARR.
2. Approve opening a Berlin office in Q1 FY27 (see strategy memo).
3. Approve a USD 5M increase in the share pool for FY27 hiring.
