# FY27 Company OKRs (draft for board approval)

## Objective 1: Grow efficiently
- KR1: Reach USD 64M ending ARR.
- KR2: Keep net revenue retention at or above 115%.
- KR3: Improve gross margin to 76% by Q4 through hosting renegotiation and caching.

## Objective 2: Win in Europe
- KR1: 60 EU customers live on the EU region by year end.
- KR2: Open the Berlin office with 25 people by Q2.
- KR3: Obtain ISO 27001 recertification with no major findings.

## Objective 3: Make the product the default for finance teams
- KR1: Analytics add-on adopted by 35% of accounts.
- KR2: Ship the close-automation module to general availability by Q3.
- KR3: Reduce time to first value for new customers from 21 days to 10.

## Objective 4: Be a place people build careers
- KR1: Regretted attrition under 8%.
- KR2: Every manager completes the new manager program within 90 days of promotion.
