# Corporate Card Policy (FIN-POL-005)

- Corporate cards are for business expenses only; cash advances are not permitted.
- Individual transaction limit USD 5,000; monthly limit USD 15,000 unless raised by the CFO.
- Card transactions must be reconciled with receipts in the expense system within 30 days of the statement date.
- Recurring software subscriptions on a card need IT approval and must be moved to a PO once they exceed USD 5,000 a year.
- Lost or stolen cards are reported to the card issuer and Finance within 24 hours.
- Cards of departing employees are cancelled on their last day; any unreconciled charges are settled from final pay where the law permits.
