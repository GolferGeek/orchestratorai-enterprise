# Month-End Close Calendar (FIN-PROC-010)

Business day (BD) numbering starts with the first business day after month end.

| Day | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| BD-2 | Cut-off reminder to all budget owners: receipts and accruals | Controller |
| BD1 | AP sub-ledger closed; last payment run of the month posted | AP lead |
| BD1 | Payroll and benefits accruals booked | Payroll |
| BD2 | Revenue recognition schedules updated; deferred revenue rolled forward | Revenue accountant |
| BD2 | Bank reconciliations for all operating accounts | Treasury |
| BD3 | Prepaid and fixed-asset schedules; depreciation posted | Staff accountant |
| BD3 | Intercompany reconciliation and eliminations | Senior accountant |
| BD4 | Flux analysis: any P&L line moving more than 10% and USD 25,000 month over month is explained | FP&A |
| BD5 | Controller review and sign-off; books locked | Controller |
| BD6 | Management reporting pack to the executive team | FP&A |

Late journal entries after BD5 require Controller approval and are logged in the close checklist.
