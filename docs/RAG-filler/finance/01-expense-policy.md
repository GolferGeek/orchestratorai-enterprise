# Employee Expense Policy (FIN-POL-001, effective 2026-01-01)

## Purpose
This policy governs reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees of Northwind Services, Inc. It applies to all employees and contractors with an approved expense account.

## General rules
- Expenses must be ordinary, necessary and directly related to company business.
- Submit expenses in the expense system within 30 days of the expense date. Expenses older than 90 days are not reimbursed except with CFO approval.
- Every expense of USD 25 or more needs an itemized receipt. A credit-card slip alone is not an itemized receipt.
- Personal expenses charged in error to a corporate card must be repaid within 15 days.

## Approval
- Expense reports are approved by the employee's direct manager.
- Reports over USD 2,500 also require the department head.
- Reports over USD 10,000 also require the CFO.
- Nobody approves their own expenses or those of their manager.

## Meals
- Meals while traveling: up to USD 75 per day, including tax and tip.
- Business meals with customers or prospects: up to USD 125 per attendee. List every attendee and their company.
- Team meals need the department head's approval in advance and are capped at USD 60 per person.
- Alcohol is reimbursed only at customer meals and never exceeds 30% of the bill.

## Non-reimbursable
Fines and penalties, personal entertainment, upgrades beyond policy, gym memberships, spouse or guest travel, and gift cards to employees.

## Mileage
Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the IRS standard rate in effect on the date of travel. Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable.
