# Vendor Onboarding (FIN-PROC-030)

Before the first PO to a new vendor:
1. The requester submits the vendor form with the legal entity name, tax ID and remit-to address.
2. Procurement screens the vendor against the sanctions list and checks it is not a related party.
3. The vendor provides a W-9 (US) or W-8BEN-E (non-US) and bank details on company letterhead.
4. AP verifies bank details by calling a known phone number on file - never a number from the email that sent the details.
5. Vendors that process customer or employee data complete the Security questionnaire.

## Bank detail changes
Any request to change a vendor's bank account is treated as potential fraud until verified by a call-back to a known contact. Payments to the vendor are held until verification is complete.
