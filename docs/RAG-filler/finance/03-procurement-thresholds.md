# Procurement and Purchase Approval Policy (FIN-POL-003)

## Purchase orders are required
Any purchase of goods or services of USD 5,000 or more needs an approved purchase order (PO) before the order is placed with the vendor. Invoices that arrive without a PO are held by Accounts Payable until one is raised and approved ("no PO, no pay").

## Approval thresholds (total PO value)
| PO value (USD) | Approvers |
|---|---|
| 5,000 - 24,999 | Budget owner |
| 25,000 - 99,999 | Budget owner and department head |
| 100,000 - 499,999 | Department head and CFO |
| 500,000 and above | CFO and CEO; board notification |

## Competitive quotes
- USD 25,000 - 99,999: two written quotes.
- USD 100,000 and above: three written quotes or a documented sole-source justification approved by Procurement.

## Splitting
Splitting a purchase into several POs to stay under a threshold is prohibited and is treated as a policy violation.

## Contracts
Purchases with a term longer than 12 months, automatic renewal, or data-processing obligations also need a contract reviewed by Legal.
