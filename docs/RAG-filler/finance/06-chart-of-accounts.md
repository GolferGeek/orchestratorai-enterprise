# Chart of Accounts Summary (FIN-REF-020)

| Range | Class | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1000-1999 | Assets | 1010 Operating cash, 1200 Accounts receivable, 1400 Prepaid expenses, 1500 Fixed assets |
| 2000-2999 | Liabilities | 2010 Accounts payable, 2100 Accrued expenses, 2300 Deferred revenue |
| 3000-3999 | Equity | 3100 Common stock, 3900 Retained earnings |
| 4000-4999 | Revenue | 4010 Subscription revenue, 4100 Professional services revenue |
| 5000-5999 | Cost of revenue | 5010 Hosting, 5100 Customer support salaries |
| 6000-6999 | Operating expenses | 6100 Salaries, 6300 Software subscriptions, 6400 Travel, 6450 Meals, 6500 Office supplies, 6600 Professional fees |

## Coding rules
- Software used by one team: 6300 with that team's department code.
- Laptops and monitors under USD 2,500 per item: 6500 (expensed). At or above USD 2,500: 1500 (capitalized).
- Conference registration: 6400 Travel, not 6600.
