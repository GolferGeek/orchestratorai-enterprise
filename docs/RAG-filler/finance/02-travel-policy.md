# Travel Policy (FIN-POL-002, effective 2026-01-01)

## Booking
- Book air, hotel and car through the company travel portal. Bookings made outside the portal are reimbursed only up to the portal's quoted price for the same itinerary.
- Book at least 14 days in advance for domestic flights and 21 days for international flights where possible.

## Air
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
- Premium economy for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Business class only for flights over 10 hours, with CFO approval.

## Lodging
- Up to USD 250 per night in tier-1 cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo) and USD 180 per night elsewhere, before tax.
- Stays over 14 consecutive nights in one city require an extended-stay rate or corporate housing.

## Ground transport
Rideshare and taxis are reimbursed. Rental cars are compact or intermediate class; decline the rental company's collision insurance, the corporate policy covers it.

## International
Travel to any country on the restricted list requires Legal and Security approval before booking. Carry a company-issued loaner laptop, not your primary device, to restricted countries.
