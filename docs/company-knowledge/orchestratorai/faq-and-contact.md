# OrchestratorAI: frequently asked questions and contact

## How do I get started?

1. Look through the website and the videos to understand the platform and its
   demo workflows.
2. Pick the business process, team or customer journey where an agent or
   workflow would help most.
3. Schedule a conversation with the OrchestratorAI team (book a demo, or email
   hello@orchestrator-ai.com).
4. The team shapes a pilot around your real data and workflows.

## Do I need to know how to code?

No coding is needed to understand the demo or evaluate use cases. How much
implementation work there is depends on your integrations, data, deployment
needs and custom workflows.

## How long does it take to build an agent?

A focused pilot can usually be scoped around a meaningful slice of work. Exact
timing depends on the workflow, data access, integrations, security review,
and model and provider requirements.

## Can I use my own LLM provider?

Yes. OrchestratorAI supports many LLM providers. You can use your own API keys
with providers you already work with, or the platform's built-in model access.
It can also run models locally.

## Is my data secure?

OrchestratorAI is built around organization isolation, roles, entitlements,
control over models and providers, observability and audit trails. How data
is handled in a particular deployment is something to discuss with the team.

## Can agents work together, or with outside agents?

Yes. Workflows coordinate several specialist agents. Through the Gatehouse,
outside AI agents can find a company's agents and call them over A2A or MCP,
with agent keys or "Log in with <company>".

## What about voice?

The platform supports voice: agents can listen and speak back. That suits
phone-like interfaces, customer service calls and accessibility.

## Is there an API?

Yes. Every agent can be called through the API, so you can build OrchestratorAI
agents into your own applications, websites and workflows.

## What happens when an agent doesn't know something?

Agents should say they are not sure rather than make things up. Escalation and
human review can be built into a workflow when the use case needs it.

## How do I book a demo?

Book a demo or a call from the website, or email hello@orchestrator-ai.com and
the team will find a time.
