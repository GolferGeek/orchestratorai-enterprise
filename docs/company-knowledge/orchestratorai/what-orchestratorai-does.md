# What OrchestratorAI does

OrchestratorAI is an enterprise AI platform. In one web app and API it
deploys useful agents, deterministic LangGraph workflows, ambient automation,
RAG-backed knowledge, and full administrative observability. It also makes a
company an agentic endpoint that outside AI agents can find and work with.

It is built for organizations that want working AI infrastructure they can
adapt to their own domain: real agents, real workflows, security,
observability, control over models and providers, and administration across
many organizations.

## The modules

- **Agents**: conversation agents for context, RAG, API, external and media
  work (images, video, infographics).
- **Workflows**: multi-step LangGraph workflows such as marketing swarm
  generation, document onboarding, legal analysis and order fulfillment, with
  human review gates, checklists and steps that wait for outside events.
- **Ambient**: event-driven automation that watches a company's systems and
  databases and starts agentic work when something changes.
- **The Gatehouse**: the front door for outside AI agents. It publishes a
  company's agents over A2A (agent-to-agent) and MCP. It offers agent keys
  and "Log in with <company>" sign-in (OAuth 2.1), and a catalog where agents
  can discover what the company offers. Every call is authenticated,
  rate-limited and audited.
- **Administration**: organizations, users, roles, entitlements, LLM usage,
  model operations, RAG management, observability and system health.

It is one unified platform app, not a set of separate product apps.

## Common uses

- **Customer service**: a 24/7 agent that answers product questions and FAQs
  and hands off to people when needed, in text and voice.
- **Sales assistance**: qualify leads, answer questions, schedule demos, and
  hand off to sales with full context.
- **Internal knowledge base**: instant answers about policies, procedures and
  institutional knowledge.
- **Document processing**: analyze contracts, invoices and reports; extract
  key information, flag issues, summarize.
- **Legal and compliance**: review documents for compliance issues, summarize
  contracts, track regulatory changes.
- **HR and onboarding**: answer questions about benefits and policies, and
  guide new hires through onboarding.
- **Marketing content**: blog posts, social media, email campaigns and ad copy
  at scale.
- **Data analysis**: connect agents to databases and analytics tools for
  conversational reporting.
- **Operations**: order intake and fulfillment workflows driven by the
  company's own database, with reviews in the tools people already use.
