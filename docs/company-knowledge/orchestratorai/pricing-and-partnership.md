# Pricing and how to work with OrchestratorAI

OrchestratorAI works as a partnership, not a self-serve price list. There are
no published prices or plan tiers.

## Ways to work together

- **Pilot program**: a focused engagement that proves value with real agents,
  workflows and business outcomes, built around the customer's own data,
  workflows, model and provider requirements, security and deployment
  constraints.
- **Full partnership**: a broader build-out of the customer's AI platform,
  including custom workflows, agents, integrations, observability and
  deployment planning.

## Questions about price

Pricing, volume, deployment and timelines depend on the engagement. The way to
get a number is a conversation with the team: book a demo or email
hello@orchestrator-ai.com. Nobody should quote a price, a plan or a discount
on OrchestratorAI's behalf.
